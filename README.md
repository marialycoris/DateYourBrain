# DateYourBrain — User Guide

Welcome to **DateYourBrain**! 🧠❤️

Finding the right course can feel overwhelming — there are thousands out there, and
most of them cost money or lead nowhere. DateYourBrain makes it simple: **swipe
through hand-picked, free-to-certify courses** the same way you'd browse a dating app,
save the ones you like, and track your progress as you go.

This guide walks you through everything you can do in the app.

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating your account
1. Open the app and tap **Create account**.
2. Enter your **email address** and a **password** (at least 8 characters).
3. Confirm your password and tap **Sign up**.
4. You'll land right on the **Discover** screen — you're ready to swipe!

### Signing back in
1. Tap **Log in** on the login screen.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Learners go straight to **Discover**; admins go to the **Dashboard**.

> **Tip:** The login screen shows demo accounts you can use to try the app right away —
> just tap them to fill in the fields.

### Navigating the app
- **Learners** will see three tabs at the bottom of the screen:
  **Discover · Tracker · Account**
- **Admins** get a sidebar menu instead, with **Dashboard, Courses, Reports**,
  plus a **View learner app** option so you can try the learner experience anytime.

## 2. For Learners

### 2.1 Discovering Courses

The **Discover** screen is a deck of course cards. Each card shows:

- 🏷️ **Trust badges** — like *Free cert*, *Accredited*, or *University-issued*
- 📌 **The outcome** — what you'll be able to do after finishing
- 🏢 **Who it's from** — Google, HubSpot, Meta, IBM, freeCodeCamp, and more
- ⏱️ **How long it takes**

**To go through the deck, simply:**

| Action | How | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| ❤️ **Save a course** | Swipe **right**, or tap the ♥ button | The course is added to your Tracker as *Interested* |
| ✕ **Skip a course** | Swipe **left**, or tap the ✕ button | It's set aside — you won't see it in your deck again |
| ℹ️ **Learn more** | Tap the **i** button | Opens the full course details |

There's no wrong choice — you can always open any saved course from your Tracker later.

When you've gone through everything, you'll see:
**"You're through today's stack."** 🎉

---

### 2.2 Course Details

Tapping **i** on any card (or any course in your Tracker) opens the detail page, where you can:

- Read the full **description** and see the **skills** you'll gain
- Check the **duration** and **trust badges**
- Read our **verification notes** — why we trust this course
- Tap **Go to course** to open it on the provider's website
- Tap **Save to tracker** if you haven't saved it yet

---

### 2.3 Your Tracker

Your **Tracker** is where all your saved courses live. Think of it as your personal
study shortlist. 📚

**Filtering:**
- Tap the status chips: **All · Interested · Ongoing · Completed**
- Or filter by **provider** (Google, HubSpot, etc.)

**Moving a course along:**
1. Find the course in your Tracker.
2. Tap the status button to move it:
   **Interested → Ongoing → Completed** ✅
3. Add **notes** — e.g. *"Week 2 done"* or *"Finish by Friday"* — by tapping the
   notes area, typing, and hitting **Save**.

This way your Tracker always reflects exactly where you are with each course.

---

### 2.4 Reporting a Problem

Spotted something wrong with a course — a broken link, a course that's no longer
free, a surprise certificate fee? Help us keep the catalog clean! 🧹

1. Open the course's detail page.
2. Tap **Report a problem**.
3. Pick what's wrong:
   - *No longer free*
   - *Link broken*
   - *Certificate requires payment*
   - *Other*
4. Add a comment if you'd like (optional).
5. Tap **Submit** — your report goes straight to our admin team's queue.

You'll see a confirmation, and the team will review it.

### 2.5 Your Account

On the **Account** tab you can:

- **See your email and role** at a glance
- **Change your password** — tap the button, enter your current password, then your
  new one twice, and save
- **Log out** — your session is ended securely on this device

## 3. For Admins

Log in with an admin account and you'll land on the **Dashboard**.
Your sidebar has: **Analytics · Courses · Reports · View learner app · Log out**.

> Admins can do everything a learner can — just click **View learner app** to switch over.

---

### 3.1 Dashboard

The **Dashboard** gives you a live snapshot of how the app is doing:

- 👥 **Registered learners** — total sign-ups
- 🟢 **Active learners (30 days)** — people who logged in recently
- 👆 **Total swipes** — how many decisions learners have made
- 📊 **Average swipes per learner** — engagement at a glance
- **Course performance table** — for each course: how many learners marked it
  *Interested*, *Ongoing*, or *Completed*, plus its **completion rate**
- **Issue reports by course** — a breakdown of what learners are reporting

Everything updates automatically — no refreshing needed after actions elsewhere.

---

### 3.2 Managing Courses

Open **Courses** in the sidebar to see the full catalog — including courses that
are hidden from learners.

**Finding a course:**
- Use the **search bar** (by title or provider)
- Toggle status chips: **Active / Inactive**

**Editing a course:**
1. Click **Edit** on any row.
2. Update any field — title, outcome line, description, provider, duration,
   skills, badges, link, or verification notes.
3. Click **Save**.

**Adding a new course:**
1. Click **+ New Course**.
2. Fill in the details.
3. Before publishing, work through the **pre-publish checklist**:
   - ✅ Provider is credible
   - ✅ Course is genuinely free
   - ✅ There's a way to verify completion
   - ✅ Content is current
4. **You must tick all four boxes before the course can go live** — this keeps
   our catalog trustworthy.
5. Click **Save** — the course appears in learners' swipe decks right away.

**Deactivating a course:**
1. Click **Deactivate** on the row.
2. Confirm in the popup.

The course disappears from swipe decks immediately, but nothing is deleted —
it stays in your list as *Inactive*.

---

### 3.3 Reviewing Reports

Open **Reports** to see everything learners have flagged, newest first.

Each report shows:
- The **course** it's about
- The **issue type** (e.g. *Link broken*)
- When it was filed and the learner's **comment**

**Triaging:**
1. Filter with the chips: **All · Open · Reviewed · Resolved**
2. When you've looked into a report, click **Mark reviewed**
3. Once it's fixed (or confirmed a non-issue), click **Mark resolved**

This keeps a clear trail of what's been handled.

## 4. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the app free?**
Yes — DateYourBrain is completely free to use.

**Are the courses free too?**
That's the whole point! We only list courses that are free to take, and most offer
a free certificate. Our admins check every course before publishing, and you can
report any course that slips through.

**What's the difference between swiping right and tapping "i"?**
Swiping right saves the course to your Tracker instantly. Tapping **i** just shows
you more info first — you can still save it from the detail page.

**I swiped a course left by mistake. Can I get it back?**
Skipped courses leave your deck, but you can still find them by searching the
Tracker if you've saved them another way — and new courses are added regularly.
When in doubt, swipe right on anything that looks interesting!

**What do the statuses in my Tracker mean?**
- **Interested** — you saved it and might take it
- **Ongoing** — you've started it
- **Completed** — you're done 🎉

**I found a broken link or a course that's no longer free. What should I do?**
Use **Report a problem** on the course page. Our admin team reviews every report.

**Can I change my password?**
Yes — go to the **Account** tab and tap **Change password**.

**How do I know a course is trustworthy?**
Look for the badges (*Accredited*, *Free cert*, *Industry cert*, *University-issued*)
and read the **verification notes** on the course page — our team documents why
each course made the cut.
